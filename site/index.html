Cut out backgrounds, clean up screenshots, and line up layers, then copy the result back into your design.

Cutout at work in two quick fixes. First an app screenshot: pasting it, removing a toast with Smart fill, trimming away the sidebar, and copying the result. Then three phone screens: pasting them, removing the background with the magic wand, cutting each phone to its own layer, aligning them and evening out the gaps, fitting the artboard, and copying the result

Paste, fix, copy back

Most fixes take only a few seconds.

Small on purpose

Just the pixel tools that come up while you design.

Magic wand

Drag to set the tolerance while you watch the selection. Edge cleanup removes color fringes.

Selections

Draw a free, fixed-ratio, or exact-size marquee. Add, subtract, invert, or move one edge by a pixel.

Smart fill

Fill a selection from the pixels around it. Works with flat colors, gradients, and textures, without AI.

Layers

Copy or cut pixels to a new layer. Duplicate, flip, and rotate layers, or drag one out as a PNG.

Align and space

Align and distribute layers with smart guides, equal spacing, and spacing handles.

Non-destructive scaling

Scale with the handles or the W and H fields. Layers keep their original pixels until you edit them.

Scans

Snap a selection to a scanned card or page, straighten the scan, and clear the paper from rounded corners.

Math in every field

Type 60/2, *2, or 50% in any number field. Arrow keys change a value by 1, or by 10 with Shift.

Keyboard first

Every command has a shortcut, and the whole app works with a keyboard or a screen reader.

Made to sit next to Figma

Copy layers in Figma and paste them straight into Cutout, with no plugin. When you're done, copy the result back.

Copy in FigmaPress Ctrl C on any frame or layers. Copy as PNG and Copy as SVG also work.
Paste and fixPress Ctrl V in Cutout, then cut, fill, erase, scale, or align.
Copy backCopy a selection, a layer, or the whole artboard, or drag a layer onto the Figma canvas.

Your images stay on your computer

Cutout works offline. It goes online only to download the images in a Figma copy you paste, and once a day to check GitHub for a new version.
